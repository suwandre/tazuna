**Changes**
- **Brace checks:** The parser and dispenser only treat `{` and `}` as braces when the token is unquoted. Quoted, backtick and heredoc tokens are treated as ordinary values. This covers brace checks in:
  - the dispenser, in `NextArg`, `NextBlock` and `ArgErr`;
  - the parser, in site-address parsing, `directives`, `directive`, `blockTokens`, `openCurlyBrace`, `closeCurlyBrace`, snippet and import tracking, and the anonymous-block check.
- **Helpers:** I added two small helpers on `Dispenser`, `isOpenBrace()` and `isCloseBrace()`. The changes are in `caddyconfig/caddyfile/dispenser.go` and `parse.go`.
