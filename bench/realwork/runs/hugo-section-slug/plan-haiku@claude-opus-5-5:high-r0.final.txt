The implementation is correct and I changed nothing. `slug` now works on sections, taxonomies and terms: setting it on a section changes the URL of that section and of everything below it. With the issue's example, the Spanish pages end up at `es/ayuda/` and `es/ayuda/como-frobear/`, while the English ones stay at `help/` and `help/how-to-frob/`.

**How it works:** a new field on the path descriptor carries the page's directory with section slugs applied (`resources/page/page_paths.go`). A small helper in `hugolib/page__paths.go` fills it by walking up through the page's parent sections. Sites that don't set `slug` on a section get the same paths as before. Permalink patterns and `url` still take precedence over slugs.
