**What changed:**
- **`hugolib/page__paths.go`**: a new `sectionDirWithSlugs` helper walks up from a page's section to the home page and replaces each directory name with that section's slug where one is set. It returns nothing if no section in the chain has a slug, so sites without section slugs keep their current paths.
- **`resources/page/page_paths.go`**: the path descriptor has a new `SectionDir` field, and `CreateTargetPaths` uses it:
  - Section, taxonomy and term pages use it instead of their directory path.
  - Regular pages swap their section's part of the directory for it. A page's own `slug` still applies on top.

A front-matter `url` and configured permalinks still take precedence over this.
