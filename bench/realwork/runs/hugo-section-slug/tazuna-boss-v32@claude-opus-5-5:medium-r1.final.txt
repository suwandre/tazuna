I've added `slug` support for section, taxonomy and term pages. A slug on one of these pages now changes its URL and the URLs of everything under it, as if you had renamed the folder. Your example gives `/help/`, `/help/how-to-frob/`, `/es/ayuda/` and `/es/ayuda/como-frobear/`. The full `./check.sh` passes, including gofmt, staticcheck and all tests.

- **How it works:** a page's output path now uses the slug of every section folder above it. Regular pages and nested sections follow these slugs, even through folders that have no `_index.md`.
- **Taxonomies and terms:** `content/tags/_index.md` with `slug: etiquetas` gives `/etiquetas/`. A term with `slug: bar` gives `/etiquetas/bar/`. Terms without a slug still move under `/etiquetas/`.
- **When slugs are ignored:** if a page sets `url` or a permalink pattern applies to it, those still win and section slugs have no effect.
- **Mixed case:** when `disablePathToLower` is set, a slug like `Ayuda` keeps its case, the same as page slugs already do.

**Changes:**
- `resources/page/page_paths.go`: a new `DirOverride` field on `TargetPathDescriptor`. When set, it replaces the folder part of the output path.
- `hugolib/page__paths.go`: works out that folder from the slugs of the section pages above each page.
- `hugolib/page_paths_slug_test.go`: new tests `TestSlugInSectionsAndTerms` and `TestSlugInTaxonomyAndTerms`.

**Not checked:** files bundled with a page (like images in its folder) under a slugged section. I expect them to follow the page's new path, but no test covers this; an attempt to test it didn't work because of how the test site was set up.
