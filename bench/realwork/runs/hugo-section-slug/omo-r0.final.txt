**What changed**
- `resources/page/page_paths.go`: `TargetPathDescriptor` has a new `SectionDir` field. When it is set and no permalink pattern or `url` applies, it replaces the path-derived section directory for sections, taxonomies and terms. For regular pages it replaces the container directory.
- `hugolib/page__paths.go`: new `sectionDirWithSlugs` swaps each path element owned by the current section or an ancestor section for that section's `slug`. It returns an empty string when no section has a slug, so sites without section slugs behave as before.
- `hugolib/site_sections_test.go`: three new tests, `TestSectionSlug`, `TestSectionSlugUglyURLs` and `TestTaxonomyAndTermSlug`.
  - `TestSectionSlug` follows your multilingual `help/` example, giving `/es/ayuda/` and `/es/ayuda/como-frobear/`. It also covers a plain page and a nested section under the slugged one.
  - `TestSectionSlugUglyURLs` covers a section slug with `uglyURLs = true`.
  - `TestTaxonomyAndTermSlug` covers a slug on a taxonomy page and on a term page.

**Behavior notes**
- A section `slug` now changes the URL of all pages under it, including sites that already had `slug` in an `_index.md` and no permalink pattern or `url` on that section. Sites using `:sectionslug` or `:sectionslugs` permalinks, or an explicit `url`, are unaffected because those take precedence.
- With `uglyURLs`, a section stays at `<slug>/index.html`, matching existing section behavior.
