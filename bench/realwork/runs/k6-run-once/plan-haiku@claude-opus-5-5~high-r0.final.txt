**What `--once` does:**
- It's available on `k6 run`, `archive`, `cloud`, `cloud run` (with or without `--local-execution`) and `cloud upload`.
- The scenario rewrite happens right after config consolidation, so archives and cloud runs get the rewritten scenario too.
- A script with no scenarios gets one `default` scenario with 1 VU and 1 iteration.
- A single scenario keeps its name, `exec`, `env`, `tags` and `options` (including browser options) and is switched to a `shared-iterations` executor with 1 VU and 1 iteration. Its other fields are dropped, and so are any `vus`, `iterations`, `duration` and `stages` options.
- Two or more scenarios fail with `--once can run a single scenario only`.
- `--once` together with `--vus`, `--iterations`, `--duration` or `--stage` fails with `--once cannot be combined with --<flag>`.
- Both errors return the invalid-config exit code.

One behaviour you might not expect: `--once` only rejects those four command-line flags. If `vus`, `iterations`, `duration` or `stages` come from environment variables (such as `K6_ITERATIONS`), the config file or the script, they are dropped without a warning.
