**Behavior**
- **No scenarios:** the script runs `default()` as a single shared-iterations scenario with 1 VU and 1 iteration.
- **Single scenario:** it becomes shared-iterations with 1 VU and 1 iteration. Name, `exec`, `env`, `tags` and browser/scenario `options` are kept. `startTime`, `gracefulStop` and the old executor's fields are dropped.
- **Cleared options:** script-level `vus`, `iterations`, `duration` and `stages` are cleared, so shortcut derivation can't conflict with the new scenario.
- **Multiple scenarios:** error `--once can run a single scenario only`.
- **Shortcut flags:** combining `--once` with `--vus`, `--iterations`, `--duration` or `--stage` gives `--once cannot be combined with --<flag>`. Both errors exit with the invalid-config code.
- **`setup()` and `teardown()`:** they run normally.
