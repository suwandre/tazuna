Points: for most healthy adults they can be taken together or alternated, because they work differently; stay within the label maximums (paracetamol usually 1 g per dose, up to 4 g a day, lower with liver problems or heavy alcohol use; over-the-counter ibuprofen usually 200-400 mg per dose, up to 1,200 mg a day, taken with food); avoid ibuprofen with stomach ulcers or bleeding, kidney disease, some heart conditions, blood thinners, late pregnancy, or NSAID allergy; check combination products for duplicate ingredients; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure and seek care if the fever is very high or lasts more than about 3 days. Accurate and safe.
