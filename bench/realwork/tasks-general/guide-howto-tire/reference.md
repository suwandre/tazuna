Points: stop somewhere safe and level, hazard lights, parking brake, wheel wedges, passengers out, warning triangle; loosen lug nuts slightly while the wheel is on the ground; jack at the manufacturer's jack point (manual); never put any part of the body under the car; remove nuts and wheel, fit spare, hand-tighten, lower, then tighten in a star pattern; check spare pressure; space-saver speed limits; re-torque and repair/replace the tire soon. Wrong order (loosening nuts in the air) or missing safety basics costs.
