Points a good answer covers: a ledger is naturally append-only (immutable entries; balances derived), which favors event sourcing or at least a double-entry journal of immutable transactions; benefits are auditability, rebuilding state, and history; costs are complexity, projections, schema evolution of events, eventual consistency of read models; CRUD balance updates lose history and risk lost updates unless guarded; a sound recommendation is immutable double-entry transactions as the source of truth, with balances as derived (possibly materialized) views, and strong consistency for posting, without necessarily adopting full event-sourcing machinery everywhere.
