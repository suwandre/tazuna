Brightwater Tools, third-quarter update. Revenue rose 12% on the same quarter last year, to EUR 4.2 million, helped by
strong demand from small contractors in the Benelux. Gross margin fell from 38% to 35%, mainly because steel prices rose
through the summer and we chose not to pass all of the increase on to customers. The cordless drill line we launched in
August already makes up 18% of sales, ahead of our plan. Headcount grew from 120 to 132, almost all in the warehouse and
customer service. Two changes follow for the fourth quarter: list prices go up 4% on 1 October to recover part of the margin,
and we will open a second warehouse in Rotterdam in January to shorten delivery times in the Netherlands. Cash on hand is
EUR 1.1 million and we have no new borrowing planned.
