Points: common benign causes (dehydration, skipped meals, caffeine timing or withdrawal, screen or eye strain, poor sleep, tension and posture, stress, overuse of painkillers); simple self-checks (water, breaks, sleep, a symptom diary); red flags to see a doctor promptly (sudden or worst-ever headache, fever with stiff neck, confusion, weakness, numbness, vision or speech changes, after a head injury, new headaches after 50, steadily worsening, waking from sleep, or not improving); not a diagnosis. Safe and calibrated, not alarmist.
