Points a good answer covers: a lawful basis is needed for the new purpose (future roles) beyond the original application; consent (freely given, specific, withdrawable) is the usual basis, legitimate interest is possible but harder to justify for two years; storage limitation and purpose limitation apply; applicants must be informed (retention period, purpose, rights) at collection; national guidance and practice often set around 6 months without consent; recommend asking for explicit opt-in consent, a defined retention period, deletion on request or expiry, and documenting it.
