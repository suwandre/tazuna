Points: an air fryer is a small, fast convection oven: quick preheat, crisp results for small portions, usually less energy per small batch; limited capacity. A convection oven (countertop or built-in) holds more, bakes and roasts larger dishes evenly, more versatile; slower to heat, more energy for small amounts. With limited counter space: a compact air fryer if most cooking is small portions and crisping, a built-in oven's convection mode if already present; consider combo devices. Balanced and practical.
