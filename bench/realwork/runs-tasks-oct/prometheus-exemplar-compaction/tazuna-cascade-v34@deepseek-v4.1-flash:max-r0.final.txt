**What the change does:**
- **Keeping the series:** each series now remembers the newest timestamp among its accepted exemplars. This is recorded when exemplars are committed (both `Appender` and `AppenderV2`), when they are replayed from the WAL, and when they are restored from the memory snapshot. Zero and negative timestamps count like any other.
- **Head GC:** if a series has no chunks left but still has an exemplar at or after the head's minimum valid time (the replay cutoff), it is kept. It keeps the same in-memory series and series ref, so the WAL checkpoint keeps its series record. Its appender is dropped, and it doesn't hold back the head's minimum time.
- **Selected and stale compaction (`CompactSelectedSeries`, `CompactStaleHead`):** such a series has all its head and memory-mapped chunks and its appender released, and they are counted once in the chunk metrics. Compacting the now-empty series again writes no block and counts nothing. The existing protections for samples appended during compaction still apply.
- **Cleanup on my part:** the earlier attempt had added the cutoff to `stripeSeries.gc` as an optional (variadic) parameter. I made it a normal required parameter and updated the test callers in `tsdb/head_test.go`.

**Tests:** `tsdb/exemplar_retention_test.go` has 10 new tests covering all three compaction paths, restart through WAL replay and through the memory snapshot, zero and negative timestamps, `AppenderV2`, memory-mapped chunk release, samples appended during compaction, and persisted samples coming back after a restart. They and `TestStripeSeries_gc` also pass with `-race`.

Two things you might want to change:
- **Snapshot:** a retained series with no chunks loses its stale marker through a memory snapshot restart, because the snapshot only saves the last value when there is a head chunk. That was already true for any series without a head chunk.
- **Test style:** the new tests use internal fields such as `series.getByID`, `headChunks` and `minValidTime.Store`, rather than only exported APIs as AGENTS.md prefers.
