Implemented symbolic-link support for ZIP and SquashFS archives.

### Changes
- Symlinks are skipped by default with a notice recommending `--links`.
- With `--links`, symlinks are exposed using the `.rclonelink` suffix.
- Symlink objects report the target length and support seek/range reads.
- `NewObject` now agrees with listings, including translated link names.
- Regular archive members ending in `.rclonelink` remain regular files.
- Added ZIP and SquashFS regression tests, including range/seek behavior.

### Verification
- `go test ./backend/archive/... -count=1`
- `go test ./backend/local -count=1`
- `go vet ./backend/archive/...`
