Implemented symbolic-link support for ZIP and squashfs archives.

- Links are skipped by default, with a notice naming `-l/--links`.
- With `--links`, they appear as `.rclonelink` objects with the target’s size and contents, including range/seek support.
- Listing and `NewObject` agree, and regular `.rclonelink` members remain unchanged.
- ZIP entries beneath archived symlinks are blocked to prevent destination traversal.
- Added regression tests, copy tests, a squashfs fixture, and documentation.

Verified successfully:
- `go test ./backend/archive/... ./backend/local ./fs/operations ./fs/sync`
- `go test -race ./backend/archive/...`
- Archive-package lint and full CLI build
- CLI smoke test confirming `copy --links` recreates a real symlink
