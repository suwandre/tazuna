Implemented archive symlink support for ZIP and SquashFS backends.

### Changes
- Honor `--links` when reading archive symlinks.
- Skip symlinks without `--links` and log a notice mentioning `-l/--links`.
- Expose enabled symlinks as `.rclonelink` objects.
- Return symlink target contents with correct size and range/seek handling.
- Ensure regular archive members ending in `.rclonelink` remain regular files.
- Keep `List` and `NewObject` behavior consistent.
- Prevent ZIP members beneath symlink paths from being copied through potentially escaping symlinks.

### Verification
Passed:

```text
go test ./backend/archive/... ./fs/operations ./fs/sync ./backend/local
go build ./...
go vet ./backend/archive/... ./fs/operations ./fs/sync ./backend/local
go test -race ./backend/archive/zip ./backend/archive/squashfs
```

Added tests covering ZIP and SquashFS listing, lookup, target reads, range/seek behavior, symlink copying, and archive traversal safety.
