Implemented symbolic-link support for the archive backend:

- ZIP and SquashFS symlinks are skipped by default with a notice mentioning `-l/--links`.
- With `--links`, symlinks are exposed using the `.rclonelink` suffix.
- Symlink object sizes equal their target lengths, and reads support seek/range options.
- Regular archive members ending in `.rclonelink` remain regular files.
- Listing and `NewObject` lookup behavior are consistent, including single-object roots.
- Symlink targets are read directly from SquashFS metadata rather than followed.
- Added ZIP and SquashFS tests covering skipped links, translated links, ranges, regular suffix names, and root lookups.

Verified with:

```text
go test ./backend/archive/...
go test ./backend/local -run 'TestSymlinkEscapeWriteThroughBlocked|TestCopySymlink'
go test ./vfs ./fs/operations ./fs/sync
```
