Implemented symlink support in the ZIP and squashfs archive backends. Without `-l/--links`, links are skipped with a notice. With it, they appear as `.rclonelink` objects whose size and contents match the target; seek and range reads are supported. `NewObject` follows the same naming rules, and regular files already ending in `.rclonelink` remain regular files.

Added tests for listing, lookup, target reads, local symlink recreation, and a hostile archive copy that leaves an outside sentinel unchanged.
