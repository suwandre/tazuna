Implemented archive symlink support for ZIP and SquashFS backends.

### Changes
- Detect archive members with `os.ModeSymlink`.
- Skip symlinks by default and log a notice referencing `--links`.
- With `--links`, expose symlinks as `.rclonelink` objects.
- Preserve symlink target contents and sizes, including range/seek reads.
- Ensure `List` and `NewObject` agree on translated-link names.
- Keep regular files ending in `.rclonelink` as regular archive members.
- Added protection regression coverage for archive symlinks that could otherwise redirect writes outside the destination.
