Implemented translated symlink support for ZIP and squashfs archives. Symlinks are skipped by default with a notice naming `-l/--links`; with links enabled, they appear with the `.rclonelink` suffix and expose their target as readable content. Lookup, range reads, and regular archive members ending in `.rclonelink` are covered. ZIP members beneath symlinks are excluded to prevent writes outside the destination.

Verified with `go test ./backend/archive/... -count=1` and `git diff --check`.
