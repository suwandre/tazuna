Implemented archive symlink support for ZIP and SquashFS backends.

### Changes
- Detect symbolic-link entries using `os.ModeSymlink`.
- Skip archive symlinks by default and log guidance to use `-l/--links`.
- With `--links`, expose symlinks as `.rclonelink` objects containing the link target.
- Preserve target size and support range/seek reads.
- Ensure `NewObject` and listings agree, including single-object archive roots.
- Keep regular members ending in `.rclonelink` as regular files.
- Added ZIP and SquashFS symlink tests, including target reads and root lookups.
- Existing local filesystem protections prevent hostile archive symlinks from escaping the destination.

### Verification
Passed:

```text

go vet ./backend/archive/... ./fs/operations ./fs/sync ./cmd/archive/...
```
