Implemented symlink support for ZIP and squashfs archives.

- Symlinks are skipped by default, with a notice naming `--links`.
- With `--links`, they appear as `.rclonelink` objects with correct target sizes and range/seek reads.
- Listings and `NewObject` use consistent names; ordinary `.rclonelink` members remain readable unchanged.
- ZIP members beneath symlinks are blocked to prevent copies from writing outside the destination.
- Added regression tests, a squashfs fixture, and documentation.

Verified with:
- `go test ./backend/archive/... ./backend/local`
- Race tests for archive and local packages
- A successful rclone build
- External archive round-trip tests and a CLI check confirming `copy --links` recreates symlinks.
