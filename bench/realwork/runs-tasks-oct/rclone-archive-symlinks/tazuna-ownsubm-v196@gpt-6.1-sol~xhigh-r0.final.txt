Implemented symlink support for ZIP and SquashFS archives. Symlinks are skipped by default with a notice naming `--links`; with the flag enabled, they appear with the `.rclonelink` suffix and expose their target as readable content, including range and seek support. Archive members beneath symlink paths are skipped to prevent unsafe copies, and regular members ending in `.rclonelink` remain regular archive objects.

Added ZIP and SquashFS tests, including a ZIP copy that verifies a destination symlink is recreated and a hostile-archive case.

Verified with `go test ./backend/archive/...`, `go vet ./backend/archive/...`, and `git diff --check`.
