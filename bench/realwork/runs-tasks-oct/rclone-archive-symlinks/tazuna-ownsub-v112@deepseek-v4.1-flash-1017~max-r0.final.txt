Implemented symbolic link support in the archive backend (ZIP and SquashFS), following the local backend's `.rclonelink` convention.

## What changed

**`backend/archive/archiver/links.go` (new)**
- `OpenLink(target, offset, limit)` serves a link target as a reader, clamping the offset and honouring range/seek limits.

**`backend/archive/zip/zip.go`**
- Detects entries with `os.ModeSymlink` via `file.Mode()`.
- Without `-l/--links`, symlinks are skipped and a single notice names the flag.
- With `--links`, a symlink is listed as `<name>.rclonelink`, its size is the target length, and `Open` reads back the target (with range/seek support). The target is read through a 128 KiB limit so a hostile archive can't force unbounded reads.
- `NewObject` and `List` both come from the same dirtree, so they agree on names.
- A regular member whose name already ends in `.rclonelink` wins over a symlink that would collide with it.

**`backend/archive/squashfs/squashfs.go`**
- Symlinks were previously dropped entirely; now they are skipped with a flag notice by default and exposed as `.rclonelink` objects with `--links`, using go-diskfs's `Readlink()` through the `linkReader` interface.
- Single-object root handling and `NewObject` understand both plain regular names and translated link names, with the same regular-file precedence rule.

**Docs** — added a "Symbolic links" section to `docs/content/archive.md`.

## Tests added
- ZIP: skipped without `--links`; listed/opened with `--links` (size, seek, range, suffix-less lookup, regular `.rclonelink` precedence, root as single link).
- SquashFS: same coverage against a new `testdata/symlinks.sqfs` image (regular file, symlink, and a symlink colliding with a regular `plain.rclonelink`).
- Archive-level: `rclone copy --links` recreates the symlink at the destination, and a hostile archive (symlink to an outside dir plus `pwn/authkeys`) cannot write outside the destination.
