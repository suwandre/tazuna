Implemented archive symlink support for ZIP and SquashFS backends.

### Changes
- Detect symlink entries in ZIP and SquashFS archives.
- Skip symlinks by default and log a notice mentioning `-l/--links`.
- With `--links`:
  - Expose symlinks using the `.rclonelink` suffix.
  - Report the target length as the object size.
  - Read the target path, including seek/range handling.
- Ensure `List` and `NewObject` agree on translated symlink names.
- Preserve regular archive members whose names end in `.rclonelink`.
- Added handling for single-object archive roots involving symlinks.
- Added hostile-archive coverage verifying symlinks cannot redirect writes outside the destination.
