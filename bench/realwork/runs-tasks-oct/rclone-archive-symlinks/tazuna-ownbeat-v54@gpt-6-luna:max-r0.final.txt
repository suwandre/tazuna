Implemented symbolic-link support for ZIP and squashfs archives. Symlinks are skipped with a `--links` notice by default; with `--links`, they appear as `.rclonelink` objects whose content is the link target. Listing and lookup preserve regular files that already end in `.rclonelink`.

Added tests for target reads, range/seek options, single-object roots, symlink copying, and protection against writes through an escaping link.

Verified with `RCLONE_CONFIG=/notfound go test ./backend/archive/...`, `go vet ./backend/archive/...`, and `git diff --check`.
