Implemented symlink support for ZIP and squashfs archives. Symlinks are skipped with a `--links` notice by default; with `--links`, they appear with the `.rclonelink` suffix and open as their target path, including range and seek support. Regular members ending in that suffix remain regular archive objects.

Added tests for listing and lookup behavior, target contents and sizes, and preventing an archive symlink from writing outside a local copy destination.

Verified with `go test ./backend/archive/...` and `git diff --check`.
