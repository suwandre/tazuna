Implemented symbolic-link support for ZIP and squashfs archives. Symlinks are skipped by default with a notice naming `-l/--links`; with links enabled, they appear as `.rclonelink` objects whose contents are the link target. Listing and `NewObject` use the same translated names, while regular files ending in `.rclonelink` remain regular files. Added coverage for range reads, copying links, name collisions, and a hostile symlink path.

Verified with `go test ./backend/archive/... ./backend/local` and `git diff --check`.
