Implemented the `lb_retry_match` expression fix.

### Changes
- CEL expressions now record the placeholders they reference from the compiled AST.
- Response-based retries are triggered only for:
  - `status_code` / `status_text`
  - response headers
  - upstream latency placeholders
  - dynamically determined placeholder names, conservatively
- Request-only expressions, escaped placeholder text, upstream-selection placeholders, and similarly named placeholders no longer retry successful responses.
- Added coverage for direct `ph(req, "...")` calls and request-only expressions.
