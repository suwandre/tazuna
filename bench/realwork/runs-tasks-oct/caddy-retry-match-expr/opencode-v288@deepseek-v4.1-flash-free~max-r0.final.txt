### Changes

- **`modules/caddyhttp/celmatcher.go`**
  - Records placeholder names referenced by the compiled CEL expression.
  - Detects both `{placeholder}` shorthand and direct `ph(req, "placeholder")` calls.
  - Treats placeholders with dynamically evaluated names conservatively as response-dependent.

- **`modules/caddyhttp/reverseproxy/reverseproxy.go`**
  - Response-based retry matching now only runs for expressions that reference response data:
    - `{http.reverse_proxy.status_code}`
    - `{http.reverse_proxy.status_text}`
    - `{http.reverse_proxy.header.*}`
    - `{http.reverse_proxy.upstream.latency}`
    - `{http.reverse_proxy.upstream.latency_ms}`
  - Request-only expressions such as `{http.request.method} == "POST"` no longer retry successful responses.
  - Escaped placeholder text and look-alike names such as `status_codes` are not treated as response data.

- **`modules/caddyhttp/reverseproxy/retries_test.go`**
  - Added coverage for shorthand and direct `ph()` response-status expressions.
