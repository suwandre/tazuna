The verification failure was environmental but real: this working directory's name contains a colon (`...flash:max-tx2f2rnc`), and `TestSnmpInitGosmi` passed `filepath.Abs(...)` to gosmi. gosmi's `AppendPath` joins MIB paths with `os.PathListSeparator` (`:`) and then splits them again with `filepath.SplitList`, so the absolute path was shredded into non-existent pieces, no MIBs were registered, the first failed `loadModule` hit the test's nil logger, and the panic followed.
