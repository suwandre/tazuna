## Summary of changes

`listen_unix.go`: introduced a reference-counted `unixSocket` that owns the shared master descriptor and filesystem path; holders get their own duplicated descriptor and are closed idempotently; the last holder unlinks the file and closes the master. Fixed the leaked `File()` descriptors and the wrong unlink name, guarded abstract sockets, and made close/listen serialization safe. Added `listen_unix_test.go` covering all review requirements.
