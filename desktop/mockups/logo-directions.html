The hand and the reins

A steady hand.

Six new logo directions for tazuna, a quiet coding agent named for the Japanese word for reins.

My take on the current icon

I like it. I’d keep the hand. The Japanese character for “hand” ties directly to the name, and its square pixels feel at home in a terminal. The loop is a clever detail, but the grid gets in its way at small sizes.

My pick: 02 Steady Hand ↓
256 & 64 px / Dock
The paper-on-ink contrast and deliberate margins work. On charcoal, the dark tile edge is quieter than the mark itself.
32 & 16 px / Small UI
At 32 px the bars still hold; at 16 px the gaps lose their rhythm and the tiny hook stops reading as a loop.
Among coding agents
The hand has more to do with the name than Claude’s burst, Codex’s prompt-cloud or Cursor’s faceted block. Its pixel texture is closer to opencode’s; the hand, not the grid, is what makes it tazuna.
